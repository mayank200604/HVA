# Agriculture and Agri-Tech Career Paths

## Scope
Career guidance for students from agriculture, horticulture, food science, and agri-technology backgrounds.

---

## Career Options

### Traditional Agriculture Roles
- Agricultural Officer
- Farm Manager
- Soil Scientist
- Extension Officer

### Agri-Tech and Innovation
- Precision farming
- Drone-based crop monitoring
- Agri data analytics
- Supply-chain optimization

### Government Sector
- ICAR
- State agriculture departments
- NABARD-related roles

---

## Skill Requirements
- Field knowledge
- Data interpretation (optional but valuable)
- Practical exposure
- Government exam preparation (for public roles)

---

## Job vs Higher Studies
- Jobs available immediately after BSc Agri
- MSc improves research, teaching, and senior roles
- MBA (Agri-business) opens leadership roles

---

## Red Flags
- Ignoring fieldwork expectations
- Assuming agriculture has no technology component
