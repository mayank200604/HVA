# Interior Design and Creative Careers

## Scope
For students interested in interior design, architecture support, visual arts, and creative industries.

---

## Career Paths

### Interior Designer
- Diploma or degree required
- Portfolio is more important than CGPA
- Client communication critical

### Visualization & 3D Design
- CAD
- SketchUp
- 3ds Max
- Blender

### Freelancing & Studio Work
- Project-based income
- Requires personal branding

---

## Skill Focus
- Visual thinking
- Software tools
- Client handling
- Portfolio building

---

## Job vs Higher Studies
- Experience > degrees
- Higher studies help in architecture-linked roles

---

## Red Flags
- Expecting fixed salary early
- Ignoring portfolio development
