# Medical and Healthcare Career Paths

## Scope
This document covers career decision-making for students interested in healthcare, medicine, allied health sciences, and clinical research.

---

## Common Career Options

### MBBS → Doctor
- Requires NEET qualification
- Long-term commitment (8–12 years including specialization)
- High responsibility and stress
- Stable and respected profession

### Allied Health Sciences
Examples:
- Physiotherapy
- Radiology Technician
- Medical Lab Technology
- Anesthesia Technology

Lower entry barrier than MBBS, faster employability.

### Healthcare Administration
- Hospital management
- Public health administration
- Health informatics

Suitable for students interested in management over clinical work.

---

## Key Decision Factors (Healthcare)

- Ability to handle long study duration
- Comfort with human biology and patient care
- Financial capacity for education
- Licensing and regulatory requirements
- Emotional resilience

---

## Job vs Higher Studies (Healthcare Context)

- Immediate jobs possible only after certifications
- Specialization strongly improves salary and growth
- Higher studies are often mandatory, not optional

---

## Red Flags
- Choosing medicine only due to family pressure
- Underestimating mental and emotional load
- Ignoring licensing requirements
