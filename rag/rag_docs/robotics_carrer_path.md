# Robotics and Core Engineering Careers

## Scope
For students from mechanical, electrical, electronics, and robotics backgrounds.

---

## Career Options

### Robotics Engineer
- Embedded systems
- Control systems
- Mechatronics

### Core Engineering Roles
- Manufacturing
- Automation
- R&D

### Research and Higher Studies
- M.Tech / MS strongly recommended
- Research labs and PSUs prefer advanced degrees

---

## Skill Requirements
- Mathematics
- Physics
- Programming (C/C++/Python)
- Hardware integration

---

## Job vs Higher Studies
- Entry-level jobs limited
- Higher studies significantly improve opportunities

---

## Red Flags
- Treating robotics as purely software
- Avoiding math and physics
