# Teaching, Education, and Academia Careers

## Scope
For students aspiring to become teachers, professors, academic researchers, or education professionals.

---

## Career Paths

### School-Level Teaching
- B.Ed mandatory
- CTET / TET qualification
- Stable but slower salary growth

### Higher Education
- Master's + NET/SET
- PhD for long-term academic roles
- Competitive and research-focused

### Education Technology (EdTech)
- Curriculum design
- Academic content development
- Instructional design

---

## Key Decision Factors
- Passion for teaching
- Comfort with repetitive academic cycles
- Research inclination
- Communication skills

---

## Job vs Higher Studies
- Higher studies almost mandatory for growth
- Research output directly impacts career progression

---

## Red Flags
- Choosing teaching as a fallback option
- Ignoring certification requirements
